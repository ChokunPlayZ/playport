Help

Connect your iPhone

  1. Open the viewer link

    Use the HTTPS link printed by the PlayPort server.

  2. Pair over Bluetooth

    On your iPhone, open Settings → Bluetooth and select the server’s device name. If asked for a PIN, enter 3939.

  3. Start CarPlay

    Keep your iPhone and server on the same Wi-Fi network, then accept the CarPlay prompt on your iPhone.
